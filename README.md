One sentence, up to 200 characters, on a small moment of wonder in everyday life: the self-checkout thanks me by name, and for one second I am grateful to the only voice in the shop that has never once mispronounced it. An AI judge compares entries two at a time, weighing whether the angle is one the reader has not met before, whether the surprise lands in a single read, and whether it stays grounded in something real.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./output python3 -P -s main.py
```

This writes the one-sentence entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, exactly one line, one sentence).
